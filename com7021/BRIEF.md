### Task 1 – Data Visualisation in python
You have recently secured a position as a data visualisation creator at a Data Insights research facility. Your line manager has asked you to create appropriate 
visualisations using the [5442] EcoMoveMobility.xlsx dataset.

EcoMove City Transport Authority operates public transport and shared mobility 
services across several European cities. The dataset includes information such as 
date, city, country, transport mode, route type, weather, event day, passengers, trips, delay minutes, on-time percentage, ticket revenue, operating cost, CO2 saved, energy consumption, customer rating, and accessibility complaints.

Your analysis should explore how sustainable urban mobility performance varies 
across time, cities, countries, transport modes, weather conditions, major event days, and route types. The organisation is particularly interested in identifying high-demand cities, weak-performing transport modes, delay patterns, accessibility issues, cost and revenue performance, customer satisfaction, and environmental impact.

Using Python, produce suitable visualisations and an interactive dashboard that 
presents a clear data-driven narrative for the target audience. Your work should 
include at least six different visualisations and one interactive dashboard. The 
dashboard may be developed using Plotly Dash, Streamlit, Panel, Bokeh, or another 
suitable Python-based visualisation framework. The visualisations should not repeat the same chart type unnecessarily and should demonstrate a range of techniques, 
such as:
1. A time-series visualisation to show mobility trends over time
2. A comparison chart to compare cities, countries, route types, or transport 
modes
3. A relationship-based visualisation, such as a scatter plot or correlation 
heatmap
4. A distribution-based visualisation, such as a histogram, box plot, or violin plot
5. A performance indicator visualisation showing measures such as revenue, 
cost, delays, satisfaction, demand, or CO2 saved
6. A dashboard visualisation combining multiple views with interactive filters

Justify why each visualisation was selected and explain how it supports the intended audience and decision-making purpose. The dashboard should help senior managers make decisions about route planning, service reliability, sustainability performance, accessibility improvements, and investment priorities.
The full investigation is to be conducted in Python. Ensure your Python code is 
submitted as an appendix to the report.

### Task 2 – Data Visualisation report
Based on your visualisation investigation in Task 1, produce a written and visually supported report that critically reflects on your analysis of the [5442] 
EcoMoveMobility.xlsx dataset.

Your report should justify the selected visualisations and interactive dashboard, 
explaining how they help technical and non-technical audiences understand transport demand, delay patterns, city-level performance, cost and revenue, customer experience, accessibility concerns, and sustainability impact.
You should present the key findings, conclusions, and evidence-based 
recommendations. The report should also briefly evaluate the effectiveness of your 
dashboard and explain how data visualisation can support business, social, 
environmental, and public-sector decision-making.
